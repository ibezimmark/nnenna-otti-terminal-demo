Talking points for the Ministry of Transport

Core value for Abia State Government

Less waiting and crowdingPassengers book specific seats from home. Tickets spread arrivals across 30-minute departure windows and remove long queues.
Financial inclusion through ABSSINThe Abia State Service Identification Number works as one transit wallet across Abia Line, ABC, Peace Mass and GUO.
Automatic levy collectionState transport levies (for example ₦500 per seat) are deducted before operators are paid, closing revenue leakage.
